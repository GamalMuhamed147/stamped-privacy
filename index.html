STAMPED · TRAVEL PASSPORT

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

In short: Stamped has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The developer never receives your data.

What the app stores

Countries, visits, cities, notes, trips, expenses, packing lists, flights, goals, your home and passport countries, and your settings. All of it is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Stamped has no server and no account, so nobody else can see it.

When the app goes online

Stamped works offline. It connects to the internet only for these features, and sends only what each one needs. No personal details or identifiers are sent.

  • Visa rules: downloads the public Passport Index dataset from GitHub. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Exchange rates: downloads a public rates file from jsDelivr (or its Cloudflare mirror). Nothing about you is sent.
  • Trip weather: sends the coordinates of the trip's city, or the city name you type in the search, to Open-Meteo. It never uses your phone's location.

Like any website you visit, these services can see your device's IP address when the app connects. Their own privacy policies apply to that.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No ads, analytics or crash reporting.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

Permissions

  • Notifications: trip reminders, scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Fingerprint / screen lock: only if you turn on the app lock. Android checks it; the app never sees your fingerprint.
  • Internet: for the features listed above.

Backups

  • Backup file: when you choose Export, the app creates a file and opens Android's share sheet. Where it goes (Drive, WhatsApp, email…) is your choice.
  • Android backup: if Android's own backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. You control this in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset all data in the app, or uninstall it. Since the developer holds no copy, there's nothing else to delete.

Children

Stamped isn't aimed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
